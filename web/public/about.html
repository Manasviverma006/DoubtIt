What is DoubtIt?

DoubtIt is a donation fraud risk analysis system that helps the public identify potentially suspicious online fundraising campaigns. It analyses campaigns for shared payment identifiers, complaint history, and review status to generate a 0–100 risk score with an interpretable breakdown. DoubtIt does not claim to prove fraud — it surfaces risk signals so donors can make informed decisions.
